## Why am I doing the ARENA curriculum?

I shipped advanced AI safety work using just-in-time learning prior to starting this curriculum: ([DocQAGroundingEval](https://github.com/andrewngo315/DocQAGroundingEval), [Secret Loyalties](https://github.com/andrewngo315/secretloyaltieshackathon)). While much of the research judgement was mine, I was quickly finding that I was losing control of my experiments at times and let agents take over, which made it harder to retake control. Studying this curriculum will give me the technical foundations that will allow me to take on future research with more informed intentions.
